Our approach

How we create value from every property

A disciplined four-step process, the same whether it's a buy-to-let in the South East or a development in Freetown.

  1. 1

    Source

    We find properties with strong potential: homes needing refurbishment, auction lots and buildings that can be repositioned.

  2. 2

    Assess

    Every opportunity is tested against strict criteria before we commit. If the numbers don't work, we walk away.

  3. 3

    Improve

    We refurbish or develop to a high standard, creating homes and spaces people genuinely want to use.

  4. 4

    Let, manage or sell

    Long-term rental, short-term letting, resale or strategic development, whichever creates the most lasting value.

Every property is weighed against seven tests

We hold our own money to the same standard we'd want for yours.

  • Condition and location of the property
  • Local housing demand
  • Refurbishment and development costs
  • Legal and regulatory requirements
  • Financing and cash-flow sustainability
  • Potential rental income or resale value
  • The long-term benefit that can be created

Questions

Frequently asked questions

What does Shammah Real Estate Holdings do?

We're a UK property investment and real estate holding company. We buy residential, commercial and mixed-use property, improve it, and then let, manage or sell it. We also manage rental properties for landlords and offer joint ventures to investors in the UK and Sierra Leone.

I'm a landlord. What would you take care of?

The day-to-day running of your property: finding and vetting tenants, collecting rent, arranging maintenance and repairs, keeping on top of compliance, and sending you regular, clear statements.

How do joint ventures work?

You partner with us on a specific project or across a portfolio. Each arrangement is set out in clear legal agreements so that our interests are aligned with yours. Terms depend on the project and are discussed openly before anything is agreed.

Where do you invest?

In the UK we focus on the South East, the Midlands and key northern cities. Internationally we invest in Sierra Leone (Freetown, Bo, Kenema and Makeni) and in carefully selected opportunities across West and East Africa.

Are returns guaranteed?

No. As with all property investment, values and rental income can go down as well as up. We share our assumptions openly and encourage every investor to take independent financial and legal advice.
