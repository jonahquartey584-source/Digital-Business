    How It Works

    A simple path from enquiry to a live, working service.

    1. 01 Step One

      Enquire

      Tell us which service(s) you're after using the enquiry form below.

    2. 02 Step Two

      Get a Quote

      We come back with a price. There's room to negotiate, especially on your first service.

    3. 03 Step Three

      Receive Your Account & Code

      Once agreed, you're issued a personal account number and an activation code tied to that service (e.g. your website).

    4. 04 Step Four

      Pay & Activate

      Pay against your account and code, and the service is switched on for you — no extra back-and-forth.
